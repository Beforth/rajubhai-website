02Our Story

Three decades.
One recipe.

Opposite Muktidham Mandir, near G. D. Somani Garden on Nasik Road — that's where it all started.

Rajesh Gokuldas Jobanputra established the Rajubhai Dabeliwale brand in 1987 with the assistance of Harshadbhai Gista in Panchvati, Nashik. In 1991, he began selling authentic Kutchi Dabeli from his hand cart. As its popularity grew, he introduced Kutchi Kadak, which quickly became a crowd favourite.

Today, Rajubhai Dabeliwale offers more than 30 varieties of Dabeli, plus Kathiyawadi Fafda made to Shri. Deepak Ramkrushna Agravat's homemade recipe — staying true to the values that started it all.
